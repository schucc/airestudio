Recommended

Use Help & Support inside Aire

The easiest way to reach us — and the best way to avoid mistakes — is through the app itself. Open Setup → Help & Support, then choose Report a Bug or Suggest an Improvement. Aire opens Mail with a pre-filled template and automatically includes your app version, macOS version, chip, memory, and AI model. Nothing from your journal is included.

Or email us directly

If you write to us from your own email client, please include enough detail for us to help — especially your system information. Wrong or missing hardware details make bugs much harder to diagnose.

Please include:

  • Aire app version (e.g. 0.1.1 (5))
  • macOS version and build number
  • Mac model and chip (e.g. Apple M4 Max)
  • Installed memory (RAM)
  • On-device AI model name, if you're using AI features
  • Steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what actually happened
  • Screenshots, if they help show the issue

The sample below shows the format our in-app links use. If you compose email manually, replace every line in the system-info block with your own Mac's details — do not copy the example hardware as-is.
