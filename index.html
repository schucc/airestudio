macOS App

Your journal. Your Mac. Your AI.

Aire is a private journaling app for Mac with a built-in AI companion that runs entirely on your device.

Warm, thoughtful replies adapt to what you write — a question, a hard day, a quiet thought. Ask your journal anything with natural-language search across weeks and months, with answers drawn only from your own words.

Features

Built for Private Reflection

Private by Design

  • No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync — your journal exists on this Mac only
  • AI replies are generated locally; your entries are never sent to a cloud service
  • Optional AES-256 encryption with a passphrase and personal recovery questions
  • Auto-lock and Lock Now to keep entries shut when you step away
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected

An AI Companion That Understands You

  • Warm, thoughtful replies that adapt to what you write
  • Powered by open on-device models you download once; nothing is streamed to the cloud
  • Natural-language search across weeks and months, drawn only from your own words
  • Retrospect: a mood calendar that quietly summarizes each day as sunny, cloudy, or rainy

A Beautiful Place to Write

  • One entry per day, edit anytime
  • A gorgeous "notebook" reader for revisiting past days
  • Six handcrafted themes and a custom Dock icon
  • Available in 11 languages — and the AI replies in yours

Bring Your World In

  • Attach photos and videos to your entries
  • On-device text recognition and AI captions make memories searchable — privately
  • Export any day or your whole journal as PDF, RTF, or text
  • Import existing journals from PDF, RTF, or TXT

Keep It All to Yourself

We do not have access to your journal entries. Everything you write stays on your Apple device. We collect nothing — except when you choose to report bugs, with descriptions of what went wrong and any screenshots you may provide.
